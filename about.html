About

The long version.

I'm Sivaharsha Sottu — most people online know me as Spirit — a second-year B.Tech student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Sai University, Chennai, in the School of Computing and Data Science.

I care about one thing more than any framework or language: understanding how things actually work. When I learn C, I learn pointers and memory before I learn shortcuts. When a portal redesign broke my userscript's selectors, I rebuilt them instead of reaching for someone else's fix. My projects are mine — designed, debugged and defended by me.

That's also why I build for real users instead of portfolios of tutorial-clone apps. My family books seva tickets with the tool I wrote. This very site started as an experiment in how far a hand-built page could go. Next up: making software that's smart enough to be worth securing.

I'm currently open to software engineering and AI internships for 2026–27 — anywhere the work is real and the learning is fundamentals-deep.
